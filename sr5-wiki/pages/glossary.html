This glossary contains common Shadowrun 5th Edition terminology. Click on any term to jump to its definition.

A

Adept
A magic user who channels mana internally to enhance their physical abilities rather than casting spells.
Armor Piercing (AP)
A weapon property that reduces the target's armor rating by the specified amount.
Astral Space
The metaphysical dimension parallel to the physical world, accessible through astral projection. Home to spirits and magical energy.
Attribute
A character's innate capability such as Strength, Agility, Logic, or Magic. Forms the basis of all dice pools.
Augmented Attribute
An attribute increased beyond its natural maximum through cyberware, bioware, or magic. Maximum augmented value is 12.
Awakened
A character who possesses magical ability (Magic attribute > 0).

B

Bioware
Organic enhancements grown from cloned tissue. Provides benefits similar to cyberware but with lower Essence cost.
Binding
The process of securing a summoned spirit to perform multiple services for a magician.
Burst Fire
A firing mode that expends 3 rounds of ammunition for +2 dice to the attack pool.

C

Chummer
Slang term for "friend" or "associate." Common runner greeting.
Commlink
A personal electronic device that serves as phone, computer, and Matrix access point.
Condition Monitor
The track of boxes that records damage taken. Separate tracks exist for Stun and Physical damage.
Counterspelling
A magical defense technique used to reduce the effectiveness of incoming spells.
Cyberware
Mechanical or electronic implants that replace or augment body parts. Reduces Essence.

D

Deck
Slang for a commlink, especially one optimized for Matrix operations.
Decker
A specialist who infiltrates the Matrix using cyberterminals and hacking software.
Dice Pool
The number of d6 dice rolled for a test, typically calculated as Attribute + Skill + Modifiers.
Direct Combat Spell
A spell that attacks a living target's body directly with magical energy. Resisted by Body + Counterspelling.
Drain
The backlash suffered when casting spells or summoning spirits. Resisted with Willpower + Logic.

E

Edge
A special attribute representing luck and destiny. Can be spent to modify dice rolls or avoid death.
Essence
A measure of a character's life force and humanity. Reduced by cyberware installation.
Extended Test
A series of rolls made over time to accomplish complex tasks. Hits accumulate toward a threshold.

F

FAD
Cheap, low-quality electronics. Short for "Factory Assembled Device."
Force
The power level of a spell or summoned spirit. Higher Force = more powerful effects but greater drain.
Full Auto
A firing mode that expends 10+ rounds for +4 dice but generates significant recoil.

G

Gamemaster (GM)
The person who runs the game, portrays NPCs, and adjudicates rules.
Glitch
A bad outcome occurring when more than half of rolled dice show 1s. Critical glitch occurs with all 1s and no hits.
Grenade
An explosive thrown or launched weapon dealing area-effect damage.

H

Hermetic
A magical tradition based on scholarly study, formulas, and elemental spirits.
Hits
Successful results on a die roll. Each 5 or 6 on a d6 counts as one hit.
Homeless
The lowest lifestyle category. Characters have no permanent residence.

I

Indirect Combat Spell
A spell that creates a physical effect (fire, lightning) that then attacks the target.
Initiative
Determines action order in combat. Calculated as Reaction + Intuition + 1d6.
Initiative Pass
A character's turn during a combat round. Most characters have 1 pass; enhanced characters may have up to 4.

J

Johnson
Slang for an employer who hires runners, typically through a middleman.
Job
A shadowrunning mission or operation.

K

Karma
Experience points earned after missions. Used to improve attributes, skills, and abilities.
Katana
A traditional Japanese sword. Popular melee weapon with excellent reach and damage.

L

Limit
The maximum number of hits achievable on a test. Types: Physical, Mental, Social, Astral.
Lifestyle
A character's standard of living. Affects monthly costs, social interactions, and available resources.
Logic
Mental attribute governing reasoning, memory, and analytical ability.

M

Magician
A full spellcaster capable of sorcery, conjuring, and enchanting.
Mana
The magical energy that permeates the astral plane and powers spells.
Matrix
The global virtual reality network. Accessed via commlinks and cyberterminals.
Metahuman
The various humanoid species including humans, elves, dwarves, orks, and trolls.
Mystic Adept
A hybrid magic user with limited spellcasting ability and adept powers.

N

Net Hits
The difference between attacker's hits and defender's hits. Determines effect magnitude.
Nuyen (¥)
The standard currency of the Sixth World.

O

Opposed Test
A contest where two parties roll against each other. Highest hits wins.
Overflow Damage
Damage that spills from the Stun track to the Physical track when Stun is full.

P

Physical Damage
Lethal damage that fills the Physical condition monitor. Heals slowly.
Power Point (PP)
The resource adepts use to purchase adept powers. Equal to Magic attribute.
Projection
The act of projecting one's consciousness into the astral plane.

R

Reach
A melee weapon property that provides bonus to hit against shorter weapons.
Resonance
A special attribute for technomancers. Represents connection to the Matrix. Cannot coexist with Magic.
Runner
A shadowrunner; someone who performs illegal operations for profit.

S

Shaman
A magician following the shamanic tradition. Uses intuition and nature spirits.
Shadowrun
An illegal operation or mission. Origin of the term "shadowrunner."
Skill
A trained ability that combines with attributes to form dice pools.
Spirit
Astral entities that can be summoned and bound by magicians.
Stun Damage
Non-lethal damage that fills the Stun condition monitor first. Heals quickly.
Summoning
The magical act of calling forth a spirit from the astral plane.

T

Technomancer
A person with innate Matrix abilities. Uses Resonance instead of Magic.
Threshold
The minimum number of hits required to succeed at a test.
Tradition
A magician's philosophical approach to magic (Hermetic or Shamanic).

U

Unarmed Combat
Fighting without weapons. Uses Strength + Unarmed Combat skill.

V

Virtual Reality (VR)
Immersive Matrix experience. Can be simsimp (simple) or full-immersion.

W

Wired Reflexes
Cyberware that grants additional Initiative passes. Available in ratings 1-3.
Willpower
Mental attribute representing mental fortitude. Used to resist drain and stun.

Y

Yen
See Nuyen.

Z

Zone
A designated area in the Matrix or physical world with specific properties.
