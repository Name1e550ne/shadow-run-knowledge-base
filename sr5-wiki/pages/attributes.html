Attributes

Attributes represent a character's innate capabilities and form the foundation of all dice pools in Shadowrun 5th Edition. There are three categories of attributes: Physical, Mental, and Special.

Physical Attributes

Physical attributes govern bodily capabilities, health, and combat effectiveness.

Attribute Description Common Uses
Strength (STR) Raw physical power and muscle mass Melee damage, lifting, climbing, unarmed combat
Agility (AGI) Dexterity, coordination, and reflexes Ranged combat, stealth, gymnastics, sleight of hand
Reaction (REA) Speed and responsiveness Initiative, defense tests, dodging
Body (BOD) Health, stamina, and physical resilience Damage resistance, toxin resistance, endurance

Strength Details

Strength determines your carrying capacity and melee damage. Each point of Strength allows you to carry 5kg without penalty.

Agility Details

Agility is crucial for runners who rely on firearms or stealth. It affects accuracy and your ability to move silently.

Reaction Details

Reaction contributes directly to your Initiative score (Reaction + Intuition). Higher Reaction means you act first in combat.

Body Details

Body is your primary defense against physical harm. It's added to armor to resist damage.

Mental Attributes

Mental attributes cover cognitive abilities, awareness, and social presence.

Attribute Description Common Uses
Logic (LOG) Reasoning, memory, and analytical ability Hacking, research, medicine, electronics
Intuition (INT) Instinct, awareness, and gut feeling Perception, initiative, tracking, survival
Willpower (WIL) Mental fortitude and determination Resisting magic/mana, stun damage, interrogation
Charisma (CHA) Personality, leadership, and charm Negotiation, leadership, etiquette, impersonation

Logic Details

Logic is essential for deckers and riggers. It determines your Matrix attribute limits and hacking effectiveness.

Intuition Details

Intuition combined with Reaction determines your Initiative. It's also critical for noticing threats and details.

Willpower Details

Willpower is your mental shield. Mages use it to drain mana, and all characters use it to resist stun and mental influence.

Charisma Details

Charisma governs all social interactions. Face characters maximize this attribute for negotiations and deception.

Special Attributes

Special attributes are only available to certain metatypes or characters with magical/matrix affinity.

Attribute Description Availability
Edge (EDG) Luck, destiny, and exceptional capability All characters
Essence (ESS) Life force and humanity All characters (modified by cyberware)
Magic (MAG) Magical ability and resonance Adepts, Magicians, Mystics
Resonance (RES) Connection to the Matrix Technomancers only

Edge Details

Edge points can be spent to:

  • Add dice to any test
  • Reroll failed dice
  • Avoid death in extreme situations
  • Act out of turn in Initiative

Essence Details

Essence starts at 6 for most metahumans. Installing cyberware reduces Essence, which can limit magical abilities and affect social interactions.

Magic Details

Magic rating determines spellcasting power, summoning strength, and adept abilities. It cannot coexist with Resonance.

Resonance Details

Resonance is the technomancer's connection to the Matrix. It functions similarly to Magic but for Matrix-based abilities.

Attribute Values & Ranges

Value Description Example
1-2 Severely impaired Injured, untrained individual
3-4 Average Typical metahuman
5-6 Above average Professional runner
7-8 Exceptional Elite specialist
9-10 Peak performance Maximum unaugmented human
11-12+ Superhuman Augmented or supernatural
Natural Maximum

The natural maximum for most attributes is 6 (metahuman average) or 7 (some troll attributes). Augmentation can increase this to 12 for physical attributes.

Augmented Attributes

Cyberware, bioware, and magic can temporarily or permanently increase attributes beyond their natural maximum.

Types of Augmentation

Type Method Essence Cost
Cyberware Muscle augmentation, reflex boosters Yes (varies)
Bioware Muscle toner, synaptic booster Minimal
Magic Sustaining spells, foci No
Drugs Temp boost chemicals No (temporary)
Augmentation Limit

The maximum augmented attribute value is 12 for physical and mental attributes. This includes all bonuses from cyberware, bioware, spells, and drugs combined.

Example Attribute Build

Street Samurai Build:
- Strength: 6 (natural) + 3 (cyberware) = 9
- Agility: 7 (natural) + 3 (cyberware) = 10
- Reaction: 6 (natural) + 3 (cyberware) = 9
- Body: 6 (natural) + 3 (cyberware) = 9
- Edge: 5
- Essence: 2.8 (reduced from 6 due to cyberware)
