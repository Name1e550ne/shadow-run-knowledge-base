Equipment is essential for any shadowrunner. This page covers weapons, armor, cybernetic enhancements, and other gear available in the Sixth World.

Weapon Notes

AP: Armor Piercing value reduces target's armor
Capacity: (c) = clip, (i) = internal, (m) = magazine
Reach: Bonus to hit against shorter-reach weapons in melee

Armor

Armor provides protection against physical and energy attacks. It adds dice to your Damage Resistance test.

Modification Effect Cost (¥)

Armor Notation

Armor is listed as Ballistic/Energy. Ballistic protects against bullets and projectiles. Energy protects against lasers, plasma, and electricity.

Essence Loss

Each point of Essence lost reduces your maximum Magic attribute by 1. If Essence reaches 0, you die.

Bioware

Bioware uses cloned tissue and genetic engineering to enhance the body. It has lower Essence cost than cyberware but is more expensive.
