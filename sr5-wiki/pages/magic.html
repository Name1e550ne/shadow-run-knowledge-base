Magic in Shadowrun 5th Edition is powered by mana from the astral plane. Magicians, adepts, and mystic adepts can harness this power through their Magic attribute.

Magic Attribute

The Magic attribute determines spellcasting ability, summoning strength, and adept powers. It cannot coexist with Resonance (technomancers).

Magic Users

Type Description Key Abilities
Magician Full spellcaster who can cast spells, summon spirits, and enchant items Sorcery, Conjuring, Enchanting
Adept Channels magic internally to enhance physical abilities Adept Powers, no spells
Mystic Adept Hybrid of magician and adept Limited spells + Adept Powers
Aspected Magician Specialist in one or two magical skills only Limited to chosen skills

Spellcasting

Spellcasting allows magicians to manipulate mana to produce magical effects.

Spellcasting Test

Dice Pool = Magic + Sorcery [Astral Limit]

Spell Types

Type Target Resistance
Direct Combat Living target Body + Counterspelling
Indirect Combat Area/object Standard defense test
Health Living target Body + Counterspelling
Illusion Senses/area Intuition + Counterspelling
Manipulation Object/area Varies
Detection Information None

Spellcasting Steps

  1. Declare spell and target
  2. Choose Force (1 to Magic rating × 2)
  3. Roll Magic + Sorcery
  4. Target resists (if applicable)
  5. Caster resists Drain
  6. Apply spell effects

Combat Spells

Combat spells are used to directly harm enemies. They come in two varieties: Direct and Indirect.

Direct Combat Spells

Direct combat spells attack the target's body directly with magical energy.

Spell Force Damage Type Description
Mana Bolt Variable Stun Basic magical projectile
Manaball Variable Stun Area-effect mana explosion
Powerbolt Variable Physical Deadly magical dart
Powerball Variable Physical Area-effect physical damage

Indirect Combat Spells

Indirect combat spells create physical effects that then attack the target (fireballs, lightning bolts, etc.).

Spell Example: Mana Bolt

Force: 6
Cast Roll: Magic + Sorcery
Defense: Body + Counterspelling
Damage: Force hits (Stun)
Drain: (Force ÷ 2) + 1

Summoning

Conjurers can summon spirits from the astral planes to serve them.

Summoning Test

Dice Pool = Magic + Conjuring + Summoning Focus [Astral Limit]

Spirit Types

Tradition Spirit Types
Hermetic Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Man, Beast
Shamanic Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Plant, Animal

Binding

After summoning, spirits must be bound to remain longer than a single task:

  • Roll Magic + Conjuring + Focus vs. Spirit Force + Resistance
  • Net hits determine services (max = summoner's Magic)
  • Failed binding = spirit may break free

Drain

Drain is the backlash from channeling magical energy. It must be resisted after casting spells or summoning.

Drain Calculation

Drain Value (DV) = (Force ÷ 2) + Modifiers

Resistance Test = Willpower + Logic [Mental Limit]

Drain Modifiers

Condition Modifier
Casting without specialization +2 DV
Quick casting -2 DV, -2 dice pool
Ritual casting -2 DV per minute of ritual
Using appropriate focus Added to dice pool

Drain Damage

If your drain resistance roll doesn't beat the DV:

  • Difference of 0 or less: No damage
  • Difference of 1-2: Stun damage
  • Difference of 3+: Physical damage
Drain Example
Magician casts Force 8 spell:
- Base Drain: 8 ÷ 2 = 4
- No modifiers: DV = 4
- Resistance: Willpower 5 + Logic 4 = 9 dice
- Rolls 5 hits
- Net: 5 - 4 = 1 hit success
- Result: No drain damage!

Adept Powers

Adepts channel magic internally to gain supernatural abilities called Adept Powers.

Common Adept Powers

Power PP Cost Effect
Improved Ability 1/rank +2 dice per rank to one skill
Improved Reflexes 3/rank +1 Initiative pass per rank
Mystic Armor 1/rank +1 armor per rank
Striking Grade 1/rank +1 damage per rank (unarmed)
Killing Hands 1 Unarmed attacks count as lethal
Enhanced Senses 0.25/rank Low-light, thermographic, etc.

Power Points

Adepts receive Power Points (PP) equal to their Magic attribute. Each power costs PP based on its rating.

Magical Traditions

Magicians follow different traditions that affect how they perceive and interact with magic.

Hermetic Tradition

Scholarly approach to magic using complex formulas, symbols, and logic.

  • Focus: Intellect and study
  • Spirits: Elemental (Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Man, Beast)
  • Style: Ceremonial, precise

Shamanic Tradition

Intuitive approach based on personal connections and nature.

  • Focus: Emotion and instinct
  • Spirits: Nature (Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Plant, Animal)
  • Style: Spontaneous, expressive
